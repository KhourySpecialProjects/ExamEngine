---
title: Conflict types
description: The seven kinds of conflict ExamEngine counts, and which ones need fixing.
---

Every schedule is checked for seven kinds of conflict. The **Conflicts** tab has one tab per kind
and repeats these definitions under **Conflict Definitions** at the bottom of the page. See
[Conflicts](/docs/reviewing/conflicts) for how to read that tab.

## Hard and soft conflicts

The double-book and per-day limit types are **hard conflicts**: the schedulers avoid them first,
and a schedule that has any isn't ready to publish. Statistics counts them under **Hard
conflicts**, and Compare lists schedules with them as not ready to publish.

Back-to-back and large-course types are **soft**: the schedulers keep them low, but a schedule can
have them and still be fine to publish.

Neither kind stops a schedule from being generated. ExamEngine places every exam it can find a
room for and reports the conflicts that remain.

## The seven types

| Conflict | Kind | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Student Double-Book** | Hard | A student is scheduled for more than one exam at the same time. Requires resolution. |
| **Student Per-Day Limit** | Hard | A student has more exams in one day than the configured maximum. |
| **Student Back-to-Back** | Soft | A student has exams in consecutive time blocks on the same day. |
| **Instructor Double-Book** | Hard | An instructor is scheduled to proctor/teach more than one exam at the same time. |
| **Instructor Per-Day Limit** | Hard | An instructor has more exams in one day than the configured maximum. |
| **Instructor Back-to-Back** | Soft | An instructor has exams in consecutive time blocks on the same day. |
| **Large Course Not Early** | Soft | Courses with 100+ students scheduled Thursday or later in the week. |

## Details

- **Double-book**: sections of one combined exam share one room, so they are one exam. Sections
  of a common exam sit in different rooms at the same time, so a student enrolled in two of them
  is always double-booked, wherever the common exam goes. The upload warns about these students
  (**Students enrolled in multiple sections of a common exam**).
- **Per-day limit**: "the configured maximum" is **Maximum Exams Per Student Per Day** or
  **Maximum Exams Per Instructor Per Day** in the schedule's settings, so the same exams can be a
  conflict in one schedule and not in another.
- **Back-to-back**: consecutive blocks are neighbors on the same day, such as 8AM-10AM and
  10:30AM-12:30PM; the 30-minute break between them doesn't help. The last block of one day and
  the first block of the next don't count. Each person is counted once per day.
- **Large course not early**: the size is the section's own enrollment, even when it is part of a
  combined exam. Any block from Monday to Wednesday counts as early.
- **Instructors**: a section without an instructor in the courses file has no instructor
  conflicts.

## Settings that affect conflicts

The settings you choose in **Generate Schedule** change how hard the schedulers try to avoid each
kind. See [Settings](/docs/generating/settings).

- **Avoid Back-to-Back Exams**: used by **Optimized** only. **Classic** always tries to avoid
  back-to-back exams, whatever the switch says.
- **Prioritize Large Classes**: used by **Classic** only, where it places the largest exams first.
  Both engines always try to keep large courses early in the week.
