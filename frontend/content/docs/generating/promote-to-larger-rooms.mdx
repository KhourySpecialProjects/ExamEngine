---
title: Promote to Larger Rooms
description: Move the fullest exams into bigger free rooms after scheduling, to leave space for alternate-seat spacing.
---

**Promote to Larger Rooms** is a switch in the Generate Schedule dialog, off by default. Both
engines use it.

## How rooms are chosen without it

Once every exam has its day and block, each block's exams get their rooms, largest exam first,
each in the smallest free room that seats it and isn't blocked out at that time. This keeps the big
rooms available for the exams that need them, but it also packs exams tightly: an exam of 40
students can end up in a 42-seat room while a 70-seat room stands empty in the same block.

## What the switch does

With the switch on, a second pass runs on each block after the rooms are assigned:

1. Among the exams in the block, take the fullest one (students ÷ seats) that can move.
2. Move it to the largest free room in that block that seats more than its current room but no
   more than twice its students.
3. Its old room becomes free for the other exams. Repeat until no exam can move.

An exam can move only to a room that is free in that block and not blocked out, and that the
large-only rule allows (see [Why exams end up unscheduled](/docs/generating/unscheduled-exams)).
The sections of a combined exam move together, as one exam.

In the example above, the 40-student exam moves from the 42-seat room into the 70-seat room (70 is
no more than 2 × 40 = 80). A 30-student exam would not go there: 70 seats is more than twice 30.

The twice-the-students cap leaves room to seat students in every other seat, without putting a
small exam in a huge hall. An exam that is already at most half full never moves.

## What it doesn't change

- No exam changes day or block, and the conflicts are exactly the same as without the switch.
- No exam is left unscheduled because of it, and no exam gets a room that's too small.
- Only rooms nobody else is using in that block are taken.

## When to use it

Turn it on when you want spacing in the exam rooms: fewer exams in rooms that are nearly full, and
the spare seats spread across the exams that need them most.

Leave it off when you want the free rooms kept free. For example, adding an exam later with
[Late add](/docs/changing/late-add) only uses rooms that are free in a block, and promotion may
have taken the larger ones.

The schedule page shows the choice as **Promote to larger rooms**: **Yes** or **No**.
